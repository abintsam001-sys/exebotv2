Offers & Coupons

Apply any of these codes during checkout to save on your fare.

How to use a coupon

  1. Select your seats and enter passenger details.
  2. On the Checkout page, find the "Enter coupon code" box.
  3. Type the code exactly (e.g. WAVE100) and click Apply.
  4. Your discount is reflected instantly in the fare summary.
